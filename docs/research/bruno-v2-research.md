## V2 critical correction

V1 incorrectly framed Madrid as "HOME" and used "THE ROAD HOME" as the primary concept.

  he could stay at his house during the weekend.
- **Madrid = FINALE** — the final round in Spain, NOT Bruno's personal home race.
- Retired "THE ROAD HOME" as primary concept.
- New internal motif: **RHYTHM / 16** — derived from Bruno's recurring public
  language about rhythm, consistency, and maximising opportunities.
